Why Local-First Matters

Privacy, durability, and control — why photo libraries belong on your machine.

Cloud albums are convenient until they aren’t: pricing changes, export friction, region lockouts, or a quiet realization that your search history lives on someone else’s servers.

Local-first means the library of record is on disk you control. Backups become your problem — and your power. LUMORA indexes folders in place, never requires an account, and keeps inference offline after you choose to download models.

That does not mean anti-cloud forever. It means the default is ownership: sync and backup stay tools you pick, not a product tax on remembering.
